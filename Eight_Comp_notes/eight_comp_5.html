Unit 5: Digital Citizenship

Complete Chapter Notes • Every Single Word from the Textbook

Digital Citizenship means using technology, the internet, and social media in a responsible and good way. Just like being a good citizen in your city, you must be a good citizen online.

Why is it Important:

Today, everyone needs to know how to use computers and phones correctly. Digital Citizenship Education (DCE) helps students learn how to:

  • Stay Safe: Keep your private information away from strangers.
  • Be Secure: Protect your devices from hackers or viruses.

Computer Ethics refers to the set of moral principles and rules that guide how people should use computers and information technology responsibly and appropriately.

In simple words, computer ethics tells us what is right and wrong behavior when using computers, the internet, and digital devices.

Example: Not sharing someone’s personal information online without their permission is part of computer ethics.

Some common ethical uses of digital technology are as follows:

  • Never use a computer or the internet to hurt or bully other people.
  • Do not do anything that is against the law (illegal) or feels wrong (immoral).
  • Do not read other people’s emails or private files without asking them first.
  • Do not try to enter other people's computer systems. It is like entering a house without an invitation.
  • Do not create or send "bad" software (like viruses) that can break or damage other computers.
  • Do not use technology to steal money or trick people.

Online Safety and Respect means using the internet in a way that keeps you safe and shows kind and polite behavior towards others.

  • Online Safety: Online safety means protecting yourself while using the internet.
  • Online Respect: Online respect means treating others nicely on the internet.

Copyright means that when a person creates something (like a story, song, or software), it belongs to them, and others cannot use it without permission. Copyright is a law that protects this work from being used by others without permission.

How creator financially benefits:

  1. Creators earn money by selling their work directly like books, songs, or software.
  2. Copyright protects creative works like books, art, plays, songs, computer software, and TV programs.
  3. They get royalties, which are small payments when others use their work.
  4. They can license their work to companies and get paid a fee for using it.
  5. Copyright laws protect their work from piracy so only they can earn money from it.

Plagiarism means taking someone else's work and saying that you made it. It is like taking a story or a drawing from another person and putting your name on it.

Explanation:

  • Plagiarism means copying someone else’s work or ideas and showing them as your own.
  • It includes copying text or pasting information from the internet into your school work.
  • It is wrong because it is like stealing and is also punishable by law.
  • You can avoid plagiarism by writing in your own words, giving credit to the source, and taking permission when needed.

The following Application Software are used:

  • Turnitin
  • Plagiarism checker X
  • ProWritingAid
  • Grammarly
  • Unicheck
  • Turnitin is an online tool used to check plagiarism in students’ work.
  • It compares your writing with books, websites, and other students’ papers to find copied content.
  • It then shows a similarity report that tells how much of the work is copied.
  • Teachers use Turnitin to make sure students write in their own words and do honest work.

Digital piracy means copying or downloading digital things (like music or movies) without the owner's permission. It is also called Internet piracy or online piracy.

Why Digital Piracy is Wrong:

  1. It is like stealing property, because digital work also belongs to its creator.
  2. It hurts creators financially by taking away their earnings.
  3. It breaks copyright laws and can lead to fines or punishment.
  4. It is dangerous for users because pirated files may contain viruses or harmful programs.

Cybercrime:

  • Cybercrime is any illegal activity done using a computer, the internet, or digital devices.
  • It includes things like hacking, stealing personal information, spreading viruses, or online bullying.
  • Cybercrime can harm people, steal money, or damage computers and networks.

Common Types of Cybercrime:

  • Hacking: Breaking into someone else’s computer or network without permission.
  • Phishing: Tricking people online to steal personal information like passwords or bank details.
  • Identity Theft: Stealing someone’s personal information to pretend to be them.
  • Spreading Viruses or Malware: Creating programs that damage computers or steal data.
  • Cyberbullying: Using the internet to bully, threaten, or hurt others.
  • Online Fraud: Cheating or scamming people to steal money online.

A hacker is a person who uses a computer to break into other people’s computer systems illegally. These people have very high skills and a deep understanding of how digital technology works. However, they use their skills to enter computers without being invited (unauthorized access).

They usually do this for one of the following reasons:

  1. To steal money by getting bank details, passwords, or credit card information.
  2. To steal or change information, like private files or important data.
  3. To cause damage by deleting files, breaking websites, or spreading malware.
  4. To spy on people by watching their activities or reading their private messages.

Computer Systems should be protected due to the following reasons:

  • To keep personal information safe (like name, address, and photos) from strangers.
  • To prevent stealing of money by protecting bank and credit card details.
  • To stop others from deleting or damaging important data like school work.
  • To avoid spying and harmful programs that can watch your activities.

Malware is a short name for "malicious software." It is a group of many bad programs that are a danger to everyone who uses a computer. People create malware to attack your privacy, spy on you, break your files, or steal your money.

Types of Malware:

  • 1. Computer Viruses: A bad program that copies itself into other programs or files. It infects your computer when you open a bad file and can damage hardware.
  • 2. Worms: A bad program that travels across a network to infect other computers. It spreads automatically and makes the internet run very slowly.
  • 3. Spyware: (SRQ 7) A program made to spy on you. It watches what you do to steal passwords and bank PIN numbers. It often enters via free downloads.
  • 4. Adware: A program that hides inside free software and shows many pop-up advertisements. It may also take your information without asking.

If your computer has malware, you will see these "symptoms" or signs:

  • The computer does not start at all or restarts by itself.
  • Strange or different messages appear on your screen.
  • Programs do not work correctly or the computer runs very slow.
  • Your folders or files are suddenly deleted.
  • Your web browser acts strangely and does not work normally.

To keep your computer safe, you must install special software that acts like a "shield":

  • Antivirus software: Identifies and removes malicious programs to prevent damage.
  • Anti-spyware: Specifically blocks and deletes software designed to steal your private information and passwords.

Use of Internet in Business:

  • It helps companies show their products to people through online ads.
  • It allows businesses to have video meetings with customers and partners.
  • It helps shops sell their products and services directly to people online.
  • It helps workers finish their daily tasks and keep track of their sales.

Use of Internet in Social Networking:

  • It helps you stay connected and talk with your family and friends.
  • It helps people share their own creative work like music, stories, and photos.
  • It allows people to join online groups to talk about new ideas together.
  • It helps businesses use social media to find and talk to new customers.

Use of Internet in Entertainment:

  • You can watch live sports, movies, and your favorite shows online.
  • You can play fun games on the internet with your friends from anywhere.
  • You can use YouTube to watch or share videos with people all over the world.
  • You can easily find and read jokes, poems, and digital magazines for free.

Use of Internet for News and Information:

  • It is the fastest way to find out what is happening all over the world.
  • You can use search engines like Google to find information about any topic.
  • It helps students find facts and data very quickly for their school projects.
  • You can get the latest news and information with just one simple click.

Use of Internet in Education:

  • It helps teachers and students stay in touch using messaging apps.
  • Teachers can easily share quizzes, lessons, and study materials with the class.
  • Students can watch online video lessons to learn new things more easily.
  • It keeps students up to date with the newest information about any subject.

Media bias means when news, articles, or information on TV or the internet favor one side or opinion and do not show the full story fairly.

  • It happens when media shows only one point of view or ignores important facts.
  • Media bias can influence people’s opinions and make them see things unfairly.

Positive Impact:

  1. It helps you stay connected with family and friends anywhere.
  2. It spreads news and information quickly.
  3. People can share ideas and help others with social problems.
  4. It allows you to show your work like music, drawings, or stories.
  5. Students can learn together in study groups and develop new skills or hobbies.

Negative Impact:

  1. People can easily spread lies, rumors, or fake news.
  2. Spending too much time online can lead to less face-to-face talking
  3. Being addicted to social media can cause health problems like stress and tiredness.
  4. Criminals can trick people online and steal money (scams and fraud).
  5. Social media can cause fights, stress, or online bullying.

Cybercrime laws are rules for everyone who uses a computer or the internet. They define acceptable behavior and legal consequences.

Need for Cybercrime Laws:

  1. They set punishments for people who commit crimes online.
  2. They keep users safe and protect personal information.
  3. They protect human rights and give police the power to catch criminals.
  4. They help countries work together to catch global criminals.

A Cyber Scout is a person who uses the internet safely and responsibly and also helps others do the same.

How a Cyber Scout helps:

  • Teaches others about online safety and cybercrime.
  • Reports suspicious or harmful online activities to parents or teachers.
  • Helps friends and family use the internet carefully and legally.

NR3C alerts users about cyber-attacks through social media ads, news in newspapers, and other media platforms so that users can secure themselves against these cyber-attacks.
