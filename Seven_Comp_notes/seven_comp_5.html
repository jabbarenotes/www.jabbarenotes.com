Unit 5: Digital Citizenship

Complete Chapter Notes • Every Single Word from the Textbook

Digital Citizenship:
Digital citizenship means using computers, internet, and ICT devices in a responsible way. A good digital citizen knows how to use technology properly and safely.

Computer Ethics:
Computer ethics means knowing what is right and what is wrong while using computers and the internet. It is a set of rules for the proper and responsible use of ICT devices.

Ethical use of computers means using computers in a safe, responsible, and respectful way.

  • Use computer in a responsible way and respect others online.
  • Do not use bad language on social media.
  • Do not use computer to harm others.
  • Do not steal, change, or destroy others’ information.
  • Do not open others’ files without permission.
  • Do not spread fake news on the internet.

Ethical issues in the digital environment are problems related to the right and wrong use of computers and the internet.

  • Using bad language or offensive content online.
  • Sharing personal information of others without consent.
  • Spreading fake news or false information.
  • Misusing computer systems to harm others.
  • Accessing or using others’ data without permission.

Online safety rules help us to protect our computer, data, and ourselves while using the internet.

  • Do not share your personal information with strangers.
  • Do not download files from untrusted or suspicious websites.
  • Make online purchases only from secure and registered websites.
  • Use a strong password with letters, numbers, and special characters.
  • Be careful while meeting people online because they may not be real.
  • Treat others in the same way you want to be treated.
  • Reply to others in a fair, responsible, and respectful way.
  • Accept and respect others’ culture and religion.
  • Respect others’ privacy.
  • Do not use racism, bad language, or insults.

Improper use of computer resources means using computers in a wrong and harmful way.

  • Using computer resources to harm other users.
  • Sending offensive or harassing messages.
  • Sharing or disclosing passwords with others.
  • Breaking the confidentiality of information.
  • Pretending to be someone else while using computer technology.

Intellectual Property Right means the legal right (copyright) of a person for their own work like software, books, or poetry.

  • The owner has the full right over their work.
  • Others cannot copy it without permission.
  • If someone copies it, the owner can take legal action.

Plagiarism means using someone else’s work or ideas as your own without permission. It means copying text or ideas and calling them your own.

Example: Copying and pasting without giving credit.

  • It is a wrong act and punishable by law.
  • It can be avoided by giving credit to the source.

Digital piracy means copying or using digital content without permission. It is a type of theft.

  • It includes copying or sharing digital content without permission.
  • When you buy content, you can use it only for yourself.
  • People who copy or use content without permission are called digital pirates.

Digital media bias means unfair reporting in digital media where journalists or news producers support or oppose someone or something.

  • Digital media includes social media, news websites, online radio, and TV channels.
  • It can be affected by background, political views, and personal experience.

Cybercrime means any illegal activity done using computer or internet. It is done by a person or group with illegal access to computer systems.

Different online cybercrimes:

  • Hacking
  • Identity theft
  • Cyber bullying
  • Cyber stalking
  • Computer viruses and malware
  • Money laundering

Privacy: Privacy means having control over your personal information and deciding what you want to share with others.

Protection from online theft of personal information:

  • Do not share your personal details with strangers online.
  • Use a strong password and keep it secret.
  • Do not open unknown links or emails.
  • Use trusted and secure websites only.
  • Keep your computer and antivirus software updated.
  • Adjust privacy settings on social media apps.

Cybercrime means illegal activities done using computers and the internet.

1. Hacking:
Hacking is entering a computer or network without permission.

  • It is done to access private or secret data.
  • Hackers can change, delete, or steal information.
  • It breaks computer security and privacy laws.

2. Identity Theft:
Identity theft is stealing someone’s personal information and using it illegally.

  • It includes stealing passwords, bank details, and card numbers.
  • Criminals use it to steal money or benefits.
  • It is done by pretending to be another person online.

3. Cyber Bullying:
Cyber bullying is using the internet to hurt or harm others.

  • It includes sending threats or abusive messages.
  • It spreads rumors or false information online.
  • It is done on social media platforms like Facebook or WhatsApp.

4. Malicious Software (Malware):
Malware is a harmful program that damages computers or networks.

  • It includes viruses, worms, spyware, and adware.
  • It can slow down or damage the system.
  • It can steal or delete important data.

5. Computer Viruses:
A computer virus is a harmful program that spreads between computers.

  • It attaches to files and spreads when opened.
  • It can damage or destroy data and files.
  • It spreads through emails, USB drives, and downloads.

6. Financial Fraud:
Financial fraud is cheating people to steal money or bank details.

  • It happens through fake calls, emails, or messages.
  • Criminals use stolen bank information to take money.
  • It can lead to identity theft and financial loss.

7. Cyberstalking:
Cyberstalking is using the internet to harass or threaten someone repeatedly.

  • It includes sending repeated threats or messages.
  • It spreads false or harmful information.
  • It causes fear and emotional stress to victims.

8. Money Laundering:
Money laundering is hiding illegal money and making it legal.

  • It hides the real source of money.
  • It converts illegal money into legal form.
  • It is done through banks and online systems.

9. Electronic Terrorism:
Electronic terrorism is using the internet to harm people or systems.

  • It spreads viruses to damage systems.
  • It attacks important computer networks.
  • It can threaten public safety and security.

10. Vandalism and Extortion:
Vandalism is damaging data, and extortion is forcing money using threats.

  • It deletes or destroys computer data.
  • Hackers lock data and demand money.
  • It is used to steal passwords and sensitive information.

Protection Against Malware:

  • Use updated antivirus software.
  • Keep system and software updated.
  • Avoid unknown emails, links, and downloads.

Major online activities are common tasks done using the internet.

1. Social Networking:
Social networking is using the internet to connect and share information with people.

  • People share photos, videos, ideas, and messages.
  • It helps to connect with friends and family worldwide.
  • It is used for communication and business promotion.

2. Online Banking:
Online banking is using the internet to manage bank accounts.

  • It is used to check account balance anytime.
  • It allows money transfer and bill payments.
  • It is done through mobile apps or websites.

3. Internet Surfing:
Internet surfing is browsing websites using a web browser.

  • It is used to search and find information.
  • It opens different websites using browsers.
  • It supports online learning and research.

4. Audio and Video Conferencing:
Audio and video conferencing is communication using voice or video over the internet.

  • It is used for online meetings and classes.
  • It allows people to see and talk from different places.
  • It saves time and travel cost.

5. Online Entertainment:
Online entertainment is using the internet for fun activities.

  • It includes watching movies, videos, and music.
  • It includes playing online games.
  • It helps to reduce stress and provides enjoyment.

6. Map Direction (GPS):
GPS is an online system that gives directions and routes.

  • It shows the correct route to reach a place.
  • It gives voice navigation instructions.
  • It shows real-time location of the user.

7. Online Education:
Online education is learning through the internet.

  • Students can study from anywhere.
  • Teachers share notes and videos online.
  • It includes live classes and virtual learning.

8. Online Auction:
Online auction is buying and selling where the highest bidder wins.

  • Products are sold through bidding.
  • Highest price wins the product.
  • It can be done anytime and anywhere.

9. Online Shopping:
Online shopping is buying goods using the internet.

  • Products are shown with pictures and details.
  • It saves time and effort.
  • Payment is done online using cards or apps.

10. Online Information Sharing:
Online information sharing is exchanging data through the internet.

  • People share photos, videos, and messages.
  • It is done through social media apps.
  • Personal data must be protected and not shared without permission.

11. Medical Assistance:
Medical assistance is getting health help through the internet.

  • Patients can consult doctors online.
  • It provides health advice and reports.
  • It saves time and helps in emergencies.

12. Online Games:
Online games are games played using the internet.

  • Players can play from different locations.
  • Many games are multiplayer.
  • It provides entertainment and fun.

Positive Impacts of Social Media:

  • People can stay connected with family and friends worldwide.
  • It is useful for advertising and selling products.
  • It is useful for learning and education.
  • It gives quick access to information.
  • It helps to increase website traffic.

Negative Impacts of Social Media:

  • It reduces face-to-face communication.
  • It increases the use of mobile phones.
  • Mobile phone radiation may be harmful to health.
  • It can be used for cyber bullying.
  • It can cause addiction and waste of time.

Medical assistance helps people get health care and advice using digital technology.

Advantages of Medical Assistance:

  • Patients can consult doctors online from anywhere.
  • It helps to get quick medical advice in real time.
  • Medical records can be shared with specialist doctors easily.
  • It saves time and travel cost for patients.
  • It improves access to healthcare services, especially in remote areas.
