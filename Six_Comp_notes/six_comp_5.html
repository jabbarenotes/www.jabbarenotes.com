Unit 5: Digital Citizenship

Complete Chapter Notes • Every Single Word from the Textbook

Ethics and safety in ICT means using computers and the internet safely and correctly. A good digital citizen:

  1. Respects others
  2. Follows rules
  3. Stays safe online

ICT security is important to keep computers, information, and users safe from hackers, viruses, and theft.

Ethics are simple rules for good behavior.

ICT Ethics means using computers and the internet in a fair and safe way. It means you do not hurt others or their work.

Digital plagiarism is copying someone else’s work—like text, pictures, music, videos, or ideas—and saying it is your own. Laws like copyright protect people’s work, and copying without permission is against the law.

Information privacy means keeping personal information safe and not sharing it without permission.

When we buy software, it is usually for one computer only. This is called a single-user license. It is like buying a seat for a bus—one ticket is only for one person!

Why it is wrong: Making software is very hard work. These laws help the people who build apps and games (the developers) to earn money for their work. If everyone copied software for free, these creators could not afford to make new and better programs for us to use.

Copyright Violation: Copyright laws protect the work of writers, artists, and software makers. If someone uses or shares their work without permission, it is called copyright violation.

Consequences: People who break these laws can face consequences, such as:

  • Paying fines
  • Getting legal action
  • Other penalties according to the law

Digital Civility: Digital civility means using computers, internet, and technology responsibly when talking or interacting with others online. It is about treating people with respect, kindness, and care while online.

Importance: Digital civility helps make the internet a safe and friendly place. It protects people from online abuse, bullying, and hate.

Cybercrime is a crime done using computers or the internet, like hacking, stealing information, or spreading viruses.

Cyber Safety: Cyber safety means using computers, internet, and social networks in a safe and responsible way.

We use computers to work, communicate with friends, and share links, but cyber criminals use computer to steal our information, like passwords, through infected files.

How to Stay Safe Online: To stay safe and protect yourself and others, you should:

  • Never use someone else's user ID or password without permission.
  • Do not view, copy, or share other people's files or work without permission.
  • Do not change or delete system files.
  • Do not use pirated software.
  • Do not send or receive infected files.

Software piracy means copying or using software without permission. It is like "stealing" software. We are not allowed to copy it and use it on other computers or sell it.

Computer users should follow ethics to use computers safely, respect others, and avoid harming people or their work.

We can keep our personal information private and confidential by following these steps:

  • Never give personal details (name, address, phone number, email, password, school name) to strangers.
  • Do not share pictures of yourself with strangers.
  • Avoid opening emails, files, or web pages from unknown people.
  • Always check with a teacher or parent before downloading anything.
  • Never respond to strangers online.
  • Tell a teacher or parent if something online makes you uncomfortable, scared, or confused.
  • Report to teacher or parent if anyone online asks to meet in person.
  • Do not share your movement plans or location on social media.
  • Do not enter online chat rooms without an adult.
  • Avoid sharing details about your life online.
  • Do not use abusive language on social media.
  • Do not buy or order anything online without parents’ supervision.
  • Never save personal information on public or shared devices.

Internet ethics means thinking about how the internet helps us live a good life and behave responsibly online.

Cyber ethics is the set of rules for responsible and safe behavior on the internet.

Just like in real life, we should not do anything online that is wrong or illegal.

Ethical Rules for Using the Internet:

  • Respect others’ opinions and accept different cultures and values.
  • Use polite and proper language when emailing or chatting.
  • Avoid arguments about religion, politics, or sensitive topics.
  • Do not share personal information on the internet.
  • Follow copyright laws when downloading software or material.
  • Use online services only for authorized purposes.
  • Avoid actions that may lead to cyber bullying or harm.

Cyber ethics are more important today because many people use computers and the internet, so following rules keeps everyone safe and responsible online.

Cyber Bullying: Cyber bullying is using technology to hurt, threaten, or embarrass someone. It includes:

  • Sending mean or rude messages, posts, tweets, or emails
  • Threatening someone online
  • Sharing personal information, pictures, or videos to hurt someone

How to Avoid Cyber Bullying:

  • Do not post anything that can hurt or embarrass others
  • Do not share someone’s personal information without permission
  • Always be kind and respectful online

Cyber safety and security means using computers and the internet safely and responsibly to protect information and stay secure online.

An Acceptable User Policy is a set of rules that tells users how they can and cannot use a network or the internet. It helps users behave properly online.

Using ICT devices too much or in the wrong way can cause health problems. Some of them are:

  • Body pain: Pain in hand, back, neck, shoulder, and arm from overuse.
  • Eye strain: Eyes get tired from using devices for a long time or from flashing lights.
  • Bad behavior: Playing violent video games too much may make students aggressive.
