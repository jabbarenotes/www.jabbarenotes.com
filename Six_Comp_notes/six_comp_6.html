Complete Chapter Notes • Every Single Word from the Textbook

Entrepreneurship is the process of starting and running your own business.

It is the ability to:

  • Develop a business
  • Organize a business
  • Run a business

The most important example of entrepreneurship is starting a new business.

An entrepreneur is a person who has a passion for a new idea. They use their skills to turn that idea into a real business.

What an Entrepreneur Do:

  • Brings New Ideas: They find what people need and make new products.
  • Creates Jobs: They start businesses that give work to other people.
  • Helps the Economy: They help the country grow by selling new things.
  • Makes Ideas Real: They turn bold thoughts into reality.

Examples of Entrepreneurs:

  • Hakim Muhammad Saeed: Hamdard Pakistan
  • Bill Gates: Microsoft
  • Steve Jobs: Apple
  • Mark Zuckerberg: Facebook

The entrepreneurial process is the way a person sets up a new business. A person can start a business from home, on the internet, or by being an innovator.

There are five main steps:

  1. Discovery: The entrepreneur looks at the market. They find what people need and come up with a new idea.
  2. Developing a Business Plan: The entrepreneur writes a plan. This plan includes the goal, the product, the location, and the cost.
  3. Resourcing: The entrepreneur finds the money. They also get things like workers and a building.
  4. Managing Company: The entrepreneur starts the company. They make the products and advertise them using the internet or social media.
  5. Harvesting: The entrepreneur sells the product to make money. They manage costs so the business can grow and make a profit.

There are different types of entrepreneurs based on where and how they work:

  1. Home-based Entrepreneur
  2. Internet-based Entrepreneur
  3. Innovators
  4. Imitators
  5. hustler

A home-based entrepreneur runs a small business from their home. The office is inside the house.

Examples: Tutoring students, writing stories (content writing), or designing. The business can be any size, as long as it is located at home.

AdvantagesDisadvantages
Low set up costNo direct contact with customers at home
No need to pay rentLack of work environment
More control over your businessLimited business growth

An internet-based entrepreneur runs their business on the internet. They set up websites to sell things, and customers buy them online.

Examples: Running a YouTube channel or doing freelancing work. These entrepreneurs take risks to start their own online company.

AdvantagesDisadvantages
It is cheap to run the business.It is hard to stay safe online.
You can talk to customers easily on social media, email, or chat.You do not see your customers face-to-face. It is hard for customers to trust you.

The internet allows businesses to reach more people, operate all the time, and grow faster compared to businesses run only from home.

Innovators are people who come up with brand new ideas and turn them into a real business. They are very hard-working and always bring new products to attract customers.

Examples: Steve Jobs, Larry Page, and Bill Gates.

AdvantagesDisadvantages
Benefit the glory of success.Must seek money (funding) for new ideas.
Create the rules and trends.Challenge of competitors.
Face low competition.Takes a long time to taste success.
Choose work hours of your choice.

Imitators are people who copy a business idea that already exists. They work very hard to make that idea a success and gain many customers.

AdvantagesDisadvantages
Easy to improve a business idea.They have to do a lot of work.
Can compare work with the original idea.They may face disappointment or rejection.
Can learn and avoid mistakes made by others.

A hustler is an entrepreneur who focuses mostly on money. They dream big and work very hard to reach their goals. They start small businesses to become bigger in the future.

A famous example of a hustler is Mark Cuban. When he was young, he sold trash bags, newspapers, and stamps to make money. Later, he built a very large and successful business.

AdvantagesDisadvantages
Their ideas are compared to the original idea.They do not always get much back for their hard work.
There is not as much risk as being an innovator.Team members may leave if they do not work as hard as the hustler.

Traditional Entrepreneurship: Traditional business is a local store. It sells products to people in the local area. Customers go to the store physically to buy things. These entrepreneurs work to create value in their community.

Digital Entrepreneurship: Digital entrepreneurship is a business where products or services are sold online. It helps to reduce poverty and create a better life.

Examples: Online courses, e-commerce (online shops), blogs, and YouTube channels.

An entrepreneur starts a business by:

  1. Thinking of an idea
  2. Making a plan
  3. Arranging money
  4. Setting up the business
  5. Launching and selling products or services
  6. Promoting the business
  7. Checking results and improving it
